The company

Every event, personally composed.

The Wedders is an event management company in Karachi, planning weddings, corporate events and birthdays for clients who want each event to feel unique, seamless and memorable.

Beyond weddings, the team plans engagements, Nikkah ceremonies, Mehndi events, daytime gatherings, baby showers and bridal showers, offering event décor, venue selection and catering services, with a personalised approach for every client.
